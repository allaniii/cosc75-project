About CentriTruck

Empowering logistics through custom, high-tech fleet management solutions.

CentriTruck is designed exclusively to meet the operational demands of Owens Trucking Services. By unifying maintenance monitoring, dispatching, and resource allocation into a single intuitive portal, we ensure maximum fleet productivity and reduced operational friction.

Our platform simplifies complex logbooks into actionable metrics, keeping drivers safe, vehicles well-maintained, and deliveries strictly on schedule.
